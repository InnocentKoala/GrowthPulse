Frequently Asked Questions

How does GrowthPulse protect my account from blocks? ▼
GrowthPulse uses a multi-layered safety architecture: randomized delays (6–14 seconds) based on natural Gaussian curves, organic daytime rest hours (~25% of active hours), an automatic sleep window between 23:00 and 08:00, and conservative daily speed ramp-ups (+20 actions/day). It also features an instant circuit-breaker that halts immediately if Instagram returns any challenge.
Do I need to share my Instagram password? ▼
No! GrowthPulse never asks for or handles your password. It runs directly inside your Google Chrome browser using your existing, authenticated Instagram web session.
How is GrowthPulse different from INSSIST? ▼
While INSSIST runs a heavy, simulated mobile phone iframe inside Chrome (which consumes large amounts of CPU and RAM), GrowthPulse runs as a lightweight, background-native extension. GrowthPulse also adds autonomous niche audience discovery, interactive follower momentum curves, and an engagement rate calculator that INSSIST does not provide.
Can I use GrowthPulse on multiple Instagram accounts? ▼
Yes! GrowthPulse automatically detects whichever Instagram profile is currently active in your browser, maintaining separate metrics, custom target sources, and activity quotas per account.
Is there a free plan available? ▼
Yes. Every new user receives a generous starter allowance of 2,000 free actions to test the discovery engine and analytics studio, with no credit card required.
